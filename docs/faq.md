# Frequently asked questions

For anyone who has stumbled onto Virtual Tavern. This is the source for the site's FAQ page.

## What is this place?

**What is Virtual Tavern?**
A small, private website where friends play party games together in real time, on their phones or computers. It's run by one person for their friends, not by a company.

**Can I play?**
Yes. The games on the front page are open: create a room, share the link, and play with your own friends. You don't need an invite from us.

**Is it free?**
Yes. There are no ads, no payments and no premium tiers.

**Why does it look so quiet?**
It's a hobby site for one group of friends. There's no public lobby or matchmaking with strangers; you play with the people you invite.

## Playing

**How do I start a game?**
Pick a game on the front page, tap **Create a room**, then send the link to your friends. Whoever creates the room is the host and starts the game.

**How many people do I need?**
It depends on the game; each game's card on the front page shows the player count. Most work best with 4–8 players.

**Do we need to be in the same room?**
No. Some games are made for talking out loud, so if you're apart, start a voice or video call alongside.

**Does it work on phones?**
Yes. Everything is designed for phones first, and works on desktops too.

**Where are the rules?**
Under **How to play** at the top of the page, and in every room through the ❓ button.

**What if I close the tab or my phone dies?**
Open the same link again on the same device and you'll be put back in your seat. The front page also offers to take you back to your last room.

**What if the host leaves?**
If the host is away for two minutes, someone who's still online automatically becomes the host.

**Can I join a game that's already started?**
You can open the link and watch. Most games let you join in at the next round.

**Can I change my name?**
Yes, with **Rename** in the room's player list.

## Accounts and privacy

**Do I need an account?**
No. You just pick a name. There are no sign-ups, emails or passwords.

**What do you store about me?**

- **In your browser:** your chosen name and a random ID, so you can rejoin your seat.
- **On the server:** the rooms and games you play, plus anonymous play statistics, such as which games were played and how they ended. These are tied to that random ID, never to who you are.

**Do you use cookies or trackers?**
No advertising or tracking cookies.

**How long do rooms last?**
A room is deleted about a week after the last time anyone was in it.

**Who can see my room?**
Only people with the link. Room links are long and unguessable, and rooms aren't listed anywhere.

**Can I delete my data?**
Clearing this site's data in your browser forgets your name and ID. Old rooms delete themselves.

## The games

**Are these games yours?**
No. They're fairly faithful versions of popular, well-loved party games, made so a group of friends can play together. They're for friends only, not for sale or wider use. All intellectual property, including the original games, names, rules, artwork and content, belongs to the original creators.
