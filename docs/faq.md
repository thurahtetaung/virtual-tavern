# Frequently asked questions

For anyone who has stumbled onto Virtual Tavern. This is the source for the site's FAQ page.

## What is this place?

**What is Virtual Tavern?**
A small, private website where friends play party games together in real time, on their phones or computers. It's run by one person for their friends, not by a company.

**Can I play?**
Yes. The games on the front page are open: create a room, share the link, and play with your own friends. You don't need an invite from us.

**Is it free?**
Yes. There are no ads, no payments and no premium tiers.

**Why is it called Virtual Tavern?**
Because game night deserves a cosy pub feel, even when your friends are three time zones away.

**Why does it look so quiet?**
It's a hobby site for one group of friends. There's no public lobby or matchmaking with strangers; you play with the people you invite.

## Playing

**How do I start a game?**
Pick a game on the front page, tap **Create a room**, then send the link to your friends. Whoever creates the room is the host and starts the game.

**How many people do I need?**
It depends on the game; each game's card on the front page shows the player count. Most work best with 4–8 players.

**Do we need to be in the same room?**
No. Some games are made for talking out loud, so if you're apart, start a voice or video call alongside.

**Does it work on phones?**
Yes. Everything is designed for phones first, and works on desktops too.

**Where are the rules?**
Under **How to play** at the top of the page, and in every room through the ❓ button.

**What if I close the tab or my phone dies?**
Open the same link again on the same device and you'll be put back in your seat. The front page also offers to take you back to your last room.

**What if the site updates in the middle of our game?**
Games are saved as you play. If the page refreshes, you're put straight back in your seat and the game carries on where it left off.

**What if the host leaves?**
If the host is away for two minutes, someone who's still online automatically becomes the host.

**Can I join a game that's already started?**
You can open the link and watch. Most games let you join in at the next round.

**Can I change my name?**
Yes, with **Rename** in the room's player list.

**My friend always wins. Is it rigged?**
The shuffles are random. Your friend is just suspiciously good. Keep an eye on them next time they're "definitely not the spy".

**Can I peek at someone's phone?**
The app can hide your cards, but it can't stop elbows. Play nice.

## Accounts and privacy

**Do I need an account?**
No. You just pick a name. There are no sign-ups, emails or passwords.

**What do you store about me?**

- **In your browser:** your chosen name and a random ID, so you can rejoin your seat.
- **On the server:** the rooms and games you play, the names you've played under, and play statistics, such as which games you played and how they ended.

**Is that anonymous?**
Anonymous, but recognisable. Everything is tied to a random ID your browser made up, not to who you are: no email, no real name, no IP address in the tavern's records. But that ID stays the same every time you play from the same browser, so the tavern can tell it's "you again" (that's how the stats know who keeps getting caught as the spy). Different phone, different ID.

**Do you use cookies or trackers?**
No advertising or tracking cookies.

**Do you sell or share my data?**
No, never. Nothing is sold, shared or used for ads.

**How long do rooms last?**
A room is deleted about a week after the last time anyone was in it.

**Who can see my room?**
Only people with the link. Room links are long and unguessable, and rooms aren't listed anywhere.

**Can I delete my data?**
Yes. Scroll to the bottom of this section, open **🧹 Forget me**, type "forget me", and press **Delete my data**. The server deletes your play history and the names it keeps for that browser, your part in past games becomes "forgotten", and your browser starts afresh. It's tucked away on purpose, so nobody does it by accident. Each device is its own player, so do it on every phone you've played on.

Just clearing this site's data in your browser makes it forget you on that device, but it doesn't delete anything on the server. Rooms delete themselves about a week after anyone was last in them.

## The back room

**What's the back room?**
A password-protected corner of the site for the owner's friends. If you don't have the password, it isn't for you. Sorry!

## The games

**Are these games yours?**
No. They're fairly faithful versions of popular, well-loved party games, made so a group of friends can play together. They're for friends only, not for sale or wider use. All intellectual property, including the original games, names, rules, artwork and content, belongs to the original creators. No copyright infringement is intended.

**Do you use AI?**
For building the site, yes: development here uses AI-assisted coding. For art, absolutely not: no AI-generated art is used here, ever. Art belongs to human artists. ❤️

**Should I buy the real games?**
Yes, please! If you enjoy a game here, the original is better: real cards in your hands, the full box, and the clever people who made it get paid for their work. Buy the physical games, the expansions and the official apps, and support the designers and artists behind them. The tavern is just a way to play with friends who are far away.
