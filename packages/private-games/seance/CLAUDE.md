# Séance

Notes for working on this game. The repo-wide rules are in the root `CLAUDE.md`, including the shared patterns every back-room game follows; this file lists only what this game adds or does differently. Update this file in the same change as any rule, id or asset change.

## Rules and boundaries

- **Séance** (`packages/private-games/seance`, id `seance`) is a cooperative vision game for 3 to 7: a silent ghost (no text input anywhere) sends picture visions so each psychic finds their suspect, place and object (or story) within 7 hours, then a shared vision and a staged vote. Never write its designers' names either. The 2–3 player variant is not built yet.
  - **Ids:** cards are numbers per kind (`vision` 1–173, `suspect`/`place` 1–31, `object` 1–32, `story` 1–20), contiguous per set in `CARD_SETS`; extra vision pools number on after 173 (`POOL_CARDS`; `pool-r` plays Reverie's picture cards, its art from the manifest's template pointing at Reverie's files). `settings.third` (`object`/`story`) plays story cards in the `object` slot; `kindOf(pillar, third)` gives the card kind. Psychics are seat colours (`blue`, `white`…); their colours replace player dots (`dot={false}`).
  - **Rules:** setup, ravens, clairvoyance tokens and levels are tables in `src/logic/setup.ts`. The server judges every guess when the ghost reveals (only once every vision is out and every psychic still searching is Ready; changing a guess takes a Ready back), the host locks, or a strict timer ends (a missing guess is wrong); verdicts hold for 12 s. A psychic can only guess while holding a vision: the first of the hour, or ones kept from a wrong guess (owner's ruling, 2026-11-13). With 3 players there are no tokens and the shared vision is face up. A vote tie goes to the furthest on the track, then the earliest finisher, then seat order. `stage` is `visions`/`reveal`/`shared`/`vote`/`done`.
  - **`view()`:** targets, the hand, the culprit, the ghost's tags and unrevealed shared cards only reach the ghost; votes are only "voted" until all are in.
  - **Seats:** through `playerPresence`, a psychic gone for 3 minutes (`AWAY_SECONDS`) counts as an empty seat (not waited on, judged or counted as a voter) until they come back. Only a psychic who left (`gone`) can be taken over or retired. The ghost's seat goes only to someone watching or a new joiner, never to a psychic. No bot.
  - **Table screen:** a watcher's 📺 Table screen is the shared poster over the room page (`SeanceTableScreen`, `PosterOverlay`: the table screen's theme, the page behind inert): the step, the hour and its clock, and a slate per psychic (portrait, name, the three finds, Searching / Ready / Done, the last verdict), with the ravens and the pile in the footer. The cards (visions, finds, verdict cards) stay on the phones (owner). At the end it stays up with the result and how far each psychic got (`SeanceTableEnd`). Leave is the only control.
  - **The ghost's screens** (`GhostVisions`, `GhostStages.tsx`, built from `GhostShell.tsx`: the ghost only; the layout below is the psychics'). Every stage has the same turn line (`GhostTurnLine`): the hour or the final, what is happening, the stage's clock and main action, the ravens, hour marks and the **Table** drawer (the table rail's contents, each psychic's past guesses, the host's tools); no tabs, step pills or separate status line. Each takes the window's free height and the full width on desktops (`GhostFrame`). **Visions** (stage `visions`): one psychic at a time. A turn line on top ("👻 Hour II · Send Ben's vision · 2 psychics left"; once every vision is out, "Listen while they decide · 1 of 3 ready", then "Everyone's ready" with Reveal the verdicts) with the hour's clock, the ravens, hour marks and a **Table** button opening a drawer (`Drawer` from `@vt/ui`) with what the table rail holds (and, on phones, the host's Lock the guesses in). The silent reminder is a first-time tip (`ghost-silent`). Under it the psychics are chips (portrait, name, current step, Waiting / ✓ Sent / ✓ Ready / ✓ Done / Left / Away; away ones last), the first still waiting chosen by default and after each send. The chosen psychic's target is big, with their guess, the visions they hold and their past guesses under it, and that kind's cards on the table beside it (their target ringed). The hand sits beside (under, on phones) with Raven; a tap opens a card full screen, Pick (on the card, where phones show a round + / ✓ mark in its corner on a 44 px square so the art stays in sight, or in the full-screen view, which then closes) chooses it, and "Send N cards to Ben" sends at once (no preview). Picks reset when another psychic is chosen. The whole screen takes the window's free height (`useFreeHeight`). On desktops the ghost's screens are the only ones that opt out of the room's 1440 px cap (`data-vt-full-width`, rule in `apps/web/src/index.css`), the hand's cards are as tall as the panel allows (`useFitSize`, no wider than 45% of the screen) and the target and table cards fill the width left with container units; on phones the cards size to their boxes with container units. Nothing scrolls at 1280×720, 1536×730, 1920×950 or on a 390×844 phone; on phones Send (or Reveal, or Throw away) sits in the `StickyBar`. **Verdicts** (`GhostReveal`, stage `reveal`): Next hour / To the final / See the end and the verdicts' clock in the turn line; a tile per psychic (portrait, Found / Missed, their guessed card, the target beside it when missed; a found one shows the card once, "Guessed their target"), under the heading of what ended the hour (`LOCKED_BY`); the hand (look only, or Raven's throw-away) beside. **Shared vision** (`GhostShared`, stage `shared`): the line-up ("This is the culprit (group n)", Compare with the group) beside the hand (about 38% of the width), Pick three, a Suspect / Place / Object tag per picked card under the hand (on phones a joined three-way switch per card, its 36 px parts edge to edge), Send the shared vision or Raven at its foot; on phones the groups fold once one is chosen. **Vote** (`GhostVote`, stage `vote`): the shared vision (a third of the width; cards still face down for the psychics dimmed, with the ghost's tags) beside the line-up with the culprit marked, the vote clock and the host's Close this round in the turn line; no hand.
  - **Phones** (psychics): the sections (My vision or Final, Others, Table) sit on the shared `TabBar`, whose dot badge marks the tab holding your move while another is open. Ready and Cast my vote sit in the fixed `StickyBar`; in the final's line-up each group on a phone is a name line over its three cards at the full width, except while you vote, when each group is one row (number and portrait, its three cards, Choose) so every group fits above the bar; while votes are cast the shared vision is a smaller row. First-time tips are a closed fold on phones.
  - **Desktops** (psychics): the current task takes the width and the table is a 20 rem rail at the right (`7fr 5fr 20rem`; `1fr 20rem` in the final), the rail fitting the window and scrolling inside (`FitArea`, More below), its labels 14 px. The psychic's vision and cards fit beside the others, and between hours (the reveal) the next cards are one row. The verdicts open the main column as a tile per psychic (portrait and words beside the guessed card, the tick on its corner), and the rail leaves them out then. In the final the line-up's groups sit two a row with two or four groups, three with three, five or six; with four groups or more the shared vision is a narrow column beside them. A psychic's line-up gets the width, with the psychics' past guesses folded into the rail. Phones: the final's line-up and a clock-lost end's What the ghost meant take the height left and scroll inside (More below).

## Assets

**Séance** (`node scripts/r2-upload.mjs seance <folder>`, dry-run first): the folder holds `vision/<n>.webp` (1–173 for the card sets, 174–341 for the `pool-a` extra visions, 342–425 for `pool-b`), `suspect/<n>.webp` and `place/<n>.webp` (1–31), `object/<n>.webp` (1–32) and `story/<n>.webp` (1–20), each with a smaller copy under `<kind>/thumb/<n>.webp`, plus `vision/back.webp`, `psychics/<colour>.webp` portraits (`blue`, `white`, `red`, `yellow`, `purple`, `brown`) and a `manifest.json` of the form `{ "back": "vision/back.webp", "sets": { "base" | "exp1" | "exp2" | "promo": { "name": "…", "description": "…" } }, "pools": { "pool-a" | "pool-b" | "pool-r": { "name": "…", "description": "…", "art": "…" } }, "psychics": { "<colour>": { "name": "…", "art": "psychics/<colour>.webp", "color": "#rrggbb" } } }`. Card numbers per set are fixed in `CARD_SETS` and the extra pools' in `POOL_CARDS` (`packages/private-games/seance/src/logic/cards.ts`). The `pool-r` pool has no files of its own: it plays Reverie's picture cards (all but Starlight's own), and its `art` points at them, `"reverie/cards/{nnn}.jpg"` (`{nnn}` is Reverie's card number padded to 3, `{n}` unpadded; the path stays under the back-room assets). Every field is optional: without the manifest, cards are numbered placeholders and psychics coloured discs. Once it is uploaded, set the game's title (next step) with the `seance` id.
