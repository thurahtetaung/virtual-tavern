# Inquest

Notes for working on this game. The repo-wide rules are in the root `CLAUDE.md`, including the shared patterns every back-room game follows; this file lists only what this game adds or does differently. Update this file in the same change as any rule, id or asset change.

## Rules and boundaries

- **Inquest** (`packages/private-games/inquest`) is a murder-deduction game for 4 to 12 (14 with Allies): a Forensic Scientist who knows the crime hints only with bullets on scene tiles, a secret Murderer (6+: an Accomplice and a Witness, host toggles) and Investigators who each get one badge to name the Murderer's exact Clue + Means card.
  - **Ids:** cards are numbers (Clue 1–200, Means 1–90; with Allies Clue 201–289, Means 91–144) and tiles fixed ids in `src/logic/content.ts` (1 Cause of Death, 2–5 Location, 6–26 scene, 27–32 events; Allies 33–39 scene, 40 Unknown Location, 41 event); items are `(tileId, 0–5)`. Events have our own names in `src/logic/names.ts`, overridden by the manifest. Never write the expansion's commercial name or the fan art's author.
  - **Flow:** `stage` is `night` (60 s, then a random pick the Murderer is told about), `collect` (round 1: Location + 6 bullets; rounds 2–3: replace a scene tile, one bullet; events resolve as sub-steps), `discuss`, `present` (host setting, off by default), `final` (the owner's table rule, on by default: after the last round everyone with a badge accuses once, in public and in seat order; the first right one wins, none means the Murderer's side; never a random accusation), `reversal` (60 s, only with a Witness after a correct solve) and `done`. Solving is open from the first bullet, outside the night and the Reversal; every badge spent ends the game. Nobody may accuse themself, and the Murderer's side is refused the true pair privately (nothing recorded).
  - **`view()`:** the crime only to the Murderer, Accomplice and current or former Forensic Scientists; the Witness gets the culprits as a sorted pair; the Forensic Scientist's drawn options and Reversal taps only to their owner. The always-visible status line is role-neutral for hidden roles.
  - **Allies expansion** (`settings.allies`, off by default, same game id; up to 14 play, `maxPlayersOf`): roles `lab-technician`, `inside-man` (Murderer's side) and `protective-detail` (needs the Witness), each a switch, leaving at least one plain Investigator. The `allies` stage comes once after round 1 (the Lab Technician's check, then the Inside Man takes a badge; the public notice never says who took it). Rulings: the Inside Man may pick anyone with a badge, himself included, knows nobody and nobody knows him, and joins the Reversal. A Change of Heart peek (`perfect-crime`) that finds an event switches that Forensic Scientist to the Murderer's side (`sideAtEnd`), secret until the end. `view()` hides the check, the peeks, the Witness (Protective Detail only) and the team (until the Reversal), tested in `allies.test.ts`.
  - **Handover:** the host can hand an absent Forensic Scientist's job to a random plain Investigator; it happens by itself when they leave for good.
  - **Table screen:** a watcher's 📺 Table screen (`TableScreen.tsx`) is the shared poster over the room page (`PosterOverlay`): who is on and their clock, the event, a slate per tile with its bulleted item and place in the order, and the suspects' names in the footer. Each tile's other items, the bullet timeline and the suspects' cards stay on the phones (owner). At the end it stays up with the result and who was who (names and roles; the crime's cards stay on the phones) (`TableEndScreen`). Leave (or Esc) is the only control.
  - **Desktop layout:** the suspects' cards run across the top with the scene's tiles and your role under them, except while the Forensic Scientist lays out the scene (`collect`, on their own screen): then the tiles and role card come first, so the item they pick and its Place bar are in view together on a laptop. The Murderer's two rows of cards are no taller than leaves the Commit button on a 720 px window.
  - **Testing:** `INQUEST_TEST_EVENTS` (a comma list of event kinds) puts those events on top of the pile.

## Assets

**Inquest** (`node scripts/r2-upload.mjs inquest <folder>`, dry-run first): the folder holds `clue/<n>.webp` (1–200) and `means/<n>.webp` (1–90) card faces, `roles/<role>.webp` (`forensic`, `murderer`, `accomplice`, `witness`, `investigator`) plus `roles/back.webp`, `tiles/<id>.webp`, and a `manifest.json` of the form `{ "roleBack": "roles/back.webp", "roles": { "<role>": { "name": "…", "art": "roles/<role>.webp", "text": "…" } }, "clue": { "1": "<name>", … }, "means": { "1": "<name>", … }, "tiles": { "<id>": { "title": "…", "items": ["…" ×6], "art": "tiles/<id>.webp" } } }`. Tile ids are fixed in `packages/private-games/inquest/src/logic/content.ts`: 1 is the Cause of Death, 2–5 the Location tiles, 6–26 the scene tiles and 27–32 the events (whose entries carry `title` and `text` instead of `items`). The Allies expansion adds Clue 201–289, Means 91–144, scene tiles 33–39, the Unknown Location tile 40, the event tile 41, and the roles `lab-technician`, `inside-man` and `protective-detail`, all in the same manifest (upload its art to the same `inquest` folder, merge its entries into `manifest.json` and bump `artVersion`). Every field is optional: without the manifest, cards read "Clue 17" / "Means 4" and tiles "Tile 7 · item 2". Once it is uploaded, set the game's title (next step) with the `inquest` id.
