# Going Once

Notes for working on this game. The repo-wide rules are in the root `CLAUDE.md`, including the shared patterns every back-room game follows; this file lists only what this game adds or does differently. Update this file in the same change as any rule, id or asset change.

## Rules and boundaries

- Phone rig SVG passes through DOMPurify before insertion; wrapper groups and transforms are React elements.
- Seat changes accept only own entries in the seat map and reject prototype keys before mutation.

- **Going Once** (`packages/private-games/going-once`, id `going-once`) is a drawing and live auction game for 3 to 6: everyone draws two pictures from secret titles, then each goes up for a live auction; secret values, wish lists, hints, screws and loans; most money wins. Never write its host character or any of its text either.
  - **Content:** title groups, hint lines and host lines are content packs (`groups`, `lines`, `host`; schemas in `src/logic/content.ts`); the deal copies only the chosen groups into state, and hint and host lines go into the game at the start (`onDeadline` has no `ctx`). Without groups the lobby refuses to start. Host lines name a player only as `{player}`.
  - **Rules that surprise:** lot order is the original's (`lotOrder`); wish lists come from a backtracking search (`setup.ts`); bids carry the lot's `seq` (a stale press is refused), at most one per player per 300 ms and none within `BID_SETTLE_MS` of any bid (`too-soon`); the soft close (`TIMINGS` by pace) restarts on every bid; screws unlock at half-time, force current + $100 on anyone but the screwer (the top bidder included), and pause the clock while they play out, then a full fresh stretch starts (`pausedUntil`); the bank opens by itself once anyone has $1,000 or less; hints (`hints.ts`) are always true when informative. Players with no credit still answer the bank, so nobody learns who is out. Leavers keep seat, cash and art. If nobody sends a drawing, everyone redraws once; nobody again ends the game (`ended: 'no-drawings'`). The pads open when the auctioneer calls for the drawings, at the drawing clock's start (`drawStart`, the view's `drawFrom`, after the voiced instructions; at once without the voice): a draw before then is refused (`not-yet`), and the pad shows locked with a line saying so.
  - **Bots** (`logic/bot.ts`) make up their mind once per price, from the lot, its `seq`, the price and their own id (`steady`, never the look's rng), so every look at one price agrees: a bot that wants the lot bids within its quick pause, otherwise it stays out until the price moves. `goingOnceBotInfo.wakeAt` wakes them when the pads open and when the last bid or a screw has landed.
  - **Practice lot:** in a room's first game (`settings.practice`), stage `practice` with a house bidder (`HOUSE_BIDDER`); nothing counts, and any player's `skip-practice` ends it for everyone. Bots leave it alone.
  - **Sound and voice** (`src/ui/audio.ts` pure, `useSound.ts`, the engine shared as `@vt/ui/sound`): cues come from the view on the server clock (`Game.since`, `Auction.since`, `HostLine.at`), each played once and dropped if over 1.5 s late. With the host's `voice` switch (off by default) a voiced game (`Game.voiced`) is timed by what is said: the server picks every clip (`logic/voice.ts`, lengths from the generated `logic/voice-lengths.ts`, rebuilt by `private-assets/tools/goingonce-voice2.py`) and clients play exactly those (`voiceCues`, `clipTimes`). Clips said to a player are `<key>-<colour>`, which is why Going Once limits rooms to its seven colours (`GAME_COLORS`). With the voice off, the text pack's lines and the pace's timing apply.
  - **`view()`:** your own titles, wish list (never which lot), messages, cash and debt; the lot's picture for everyone but its title, value and artist only at Sold; strokes only for the lot on the easel; others' cash and who had each lot wished (`knew`) only at the end.
  - **Phone layout:** during the lots the room's tab bar has Auction / Wish list / Hints / Lots (`GO_TABS` in `Auction.tsx`; other tabs hide the lot rather than unmount it, so its moments keep their place); the bid buttons, the bank's answers and the drawing pad's Send sit in the sticky bar with their refusals; the phone picture is at most 220 px tall; the end uses `EndTabs` (Scores / Awards / Gallery), and on desktops `EndLayout` puts the finale (the top lot's drawing) beside the scores and gallery; phones show that drawing small in place of the emblem. On desktops the Sold card sits at the top of the bidding column, and at Sold Screw and the bank's call move under your cash while the dead bid buttons go; the lot's picture (the practice lot's too) and the drawing pad are sized with `useFitSize` (the pad on phones too, within the play area). The drawing screen is the shared pad's layout: the sheet on the page, as big as the window allows, beside one labelled tool column (Pen, Size, Fix a mistake, then the note, the clock and Send); on phones the sheet, then one tool strip, with the clock and Send in the sticky bar. The title is in the status line only, and there is no painting frame. The wish list, hints and lots column scrolls inside the window with More below. While the auction pauses (half-time, the bank) the lots so far show under the card. The table screen's final standings show each player's priciest lot above their total.
  - **Table screen and show:** the first game on the show layer (`@vt/game-sdk/show`, `@vt/ui/show`). `moments(view, ctx)` in `src/ui/show.ts` is pure, on the same server times as `audio.ts`'s cues; `GoingOnceStage` (`Stage.tsx`) is the TV, `PhoneShow.tsx` the phones (`phone-quiet` with a table screen, `phone-full` without). Rigs and textures are private (the manifest's `show`). A paired table screen takes the sound until the host picks a mode (`soundModeIn`, `settings.soundChosen`).
  - **Without rigs** the table screen is the shared poster (`Plain` in `Stage.tsx`): the lot, its bid (with the top bidder and the last few bids beside it) or sale, the stage words and the auctioneer's line.
  - **Testing:** `GOING_ONCE_TEST_RIG=1` lets the host rig the next game from the lobby (fixed deal, halved clocks, low cash so the bank comes).

## Assets

**Going Once** (`node scripts/r2-upload.mjs going-once <folder>`, dry-run first): the house pictures (one per title, for lots nobody drew) in `house/<key>.webp`, the UI pictures in `ui/` (`buyer-0`…`buyer-2` and `bank`: the hint senders' portraits; `bank-bite` for the end's debt step; `gallery`, `easel`, `lot`: stage art for desktop and TV; `handle` and `paddles/<shape>.webp` silhouettes), the sound files in `audio/`, and a `manifest.json` of the form `{ "names": { "bank": "…", "buyers": ["…", "…", "…"] }, "house": { "<house key>": "house/<key>.webp" }, "ui": { "buyer-0": "ui/buyer-0.webp", …, "paddles": ["ui/paddles/….webp", …] }, "audio": { "music": {…}, "sfx": {…}, "voice": {…}, "durations": {…}, "text": {…} }, "credit": { "text": "…", "url": "https://…" } }` (shape in `packages/private-games/going-once/src/logic/manifest.ts`; `names.buyers` are the three art buyers who send the tips, in portrait order). `audio` lists clips by key, each a list to pick from: `music` per part of the game (`lobby`, `draw`, `auction`, `screw`, `bank`, `scores`), `sfx` per beat (`bid`, `bidder-voice` with one yelp per paddle, `going-once`, `sold`, `message` with one ring per sender…), `voice` per auctioneer moment (`bid-<amount>`, `opening`, `going-once`, `sold`, `unsold`…), with every clip's length in ms (`durations`) and what each voice clip says (`text`, so the voice plays the clip matching the line on screen); `packages/private-games/going-once/src/ui/audio.ts` names every key it plays. A house key is a title's entry in the `house` list of its title group (the groups content pack, next item); groups without one use the title's prompt id, `<group id>_<index>`. Every field is optional: without the manifest, house lots show a "House picture" placeholder and the bank keeps its plain name. The title groups and hint lines themselves are content packs, imported separately (Day to day). Its table screen and phone show come from `show/` (`<game>.stage.rig.json`, `<game>.phone.rig.json` and their `tex-*.webp` textures, exported from the owner's copy with the private show tools) and the manifest's `show` block: `{ "stage": { "rig": "show/going-once.stage.rig.json", "phases": { "lobby": [files…], "draw": […], "practice": […], "auction": […], "end": […] } }, "phone": { "rig": "show/going-once.phone.rig.json", "phases": { "draw": […], "practice": […], "auction": […] } } }`, each phase listing the files it draws (a device fetches a phase's files into Cache Storage and loads the rig when its phase lists it). Upload the new files and merge the block into the live manifest (download it, merge, dry-run, upload only the new keys). Without them the table screen shows the room's words only and phones show no stamp. Once the art is uploaded, set the title with the `going-once` id.
