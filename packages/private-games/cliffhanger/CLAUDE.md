# Cliffhanger

Notes for working on this game. The repo-wide rules are in the root `CLAUDE.md`, including the shared patterns every back-room game follows; this file lists only what this game adds or does differently. Update this file in the same change as any rule, id or asset change.

## Rules and boundaries

- **Cliffhanger** (`packages/private-games/cliffhanger`, id `cliffhanger`) is a film face-off and describing game for 4 to 16 in two teams (Popcorn 🍿 and Nachos 🌮; newcomers join the smaller one). Never write its film card lists either.
  - **Content packs only:** `films`/`shows` (`{title, genre, tier?}`) and `categories`/`tv-categories` (strings), schemas in `src/logic/content.ts`. The manifest's genre tapes (and the face-off card) are portrait art shown turned onto their side (`SideArt`, boxes mapped by `turnBox`), so every title reads left to right; plain landscape tapes in each genre's colour without it.
  - **No app judging:** players decide out loud whether an answer counts or a describer slipped; nothing pauses a face-off.
  - **Rules:** a face-off is a rally of Said it! presses, each resetting the answer clock; running out loses. The winner draws 6 and keeps 3, the rest go to the loser (No advantage: 5 each). Each square says how to describe (`one-word`, `quote`, `act`, `describe-badly`, `cast`; Voice call's third square and a player on a call get the host's `actAlt` for Act it: Describe it badly, the default, or Name the cast). The winner's team describes first; all three got opens the other side's cards to steal; a turn with nothing left is skipped. After a round, three of a genre may be traded for one of the other team's; the trailing team may pick the next category; sudden death is an option. The host picks the goal's genres (at least 4; the film presets set them) and 1 or 2 of each (`goalFor`; a saved `goal` alone still reads as its preset); off genres leave the deck, and Start needs `titlesNeeded` titles per picked genre.
  - **`view()`:** a card reaches its describer (only them while it is described), its own team after its turn, the stealing describer once stealing opens, and everyone once got or in the trade; never the team still guessing it, watchers or the table screen.
  - **Sound:** effects only, where the host picks (a paired table screen until then): the clock ticks, three hurry loops over a turn's last 10 s, the buzzer, stings; the describer's phone buzzes at 5 s.
  - **Table screen:** the shared poster (`Stage.tsx`): each team's genres, tapes and people on its side, the moment in the middle (the face-off's category card, the turn's squares as slates, the 120 px clock). The tapes lie flat, side by side, never upright (owner).
  - **Testing:** `CLIFFHANGER_TEST_DECK=1` deals titles and categories in the packs' own order.

## Assets

**Cliffhanger** (`node scripts/r2-upload.mjs cliffhanger <folder>`, dry-run first): the genre tapes in `tapes/` (portrait, 538×740, with `tapes/thumb/` copies), the face-off card (536×740, + thumb), the board, the sign, the box, the app pictures (`go`, `timesUp`, `faceoffTitle`, `roundTitle`, `pressStart`, `pressReset`, `buzzer`, `background`), the sound clips, and a `manifest.json` of the form `{ "title", "credit": "…" or { "title", "paragraphs", "links" }, "box", "boxReturns", "board", "sign", "genres": { "<film genre>": { "name", "tape", "thumb", "labelBox": [x, y, w, h] } }, "tvGenres": { "<tv genre>": { "name", "tape", "thumb", "labelBox", "genreBox" } }, "faceoff": { "card", "thumb", "textBox" }, "app": { … }, "audio": { "<cue>": { "file", "fallback", "length" } }, "names": { "genres", "tvGenres", "styles" } }` (shape in `packages/private-games/cliffhanger/src/logic/manifest.ts`; boxes are fractions of the picture, and titles are printed turned 90° clockwise in `labelBox`). Cues are `tick`, `hurry`, `hurry2`, `hurry3`, `buzzer`, `press`, `got`, `pass`, `countdown`, `steal`, `genre` and `win`; `fallback` is played where the browser can't play `file`. Every field is optional: without it, tapes are drawn in each genre's colour and the game is silent. The titles and categories are content packs, never in the repository: `ADMIN_PASSWORD=… node scripts/import-content-pack.mjs <url> cliffhanger films <file.json>` with `{ "films": [{ "title", "genre", "tier"? }] }` (genres `action`, `classics`, `drama`, `comedy`, `horror`, `family`, `animation`, `scifi`, then `heart`, `otherworld`, `remakes`, `adult`; `tier` is `easy` or `medium`), `categories` with `{ "categories": ["…"] }`, and the TV deck's `shows` (genres `action`, `classics`, `drama`, `comedy`, `crime`, `reality`, `animation`, `scifi`) and `tv-categories` the same way. The host picks which genres count, and a game starts only when every picked genre has at least 10 titles (20 for two of each) in the chosen packs. Once it is uploaded, set the title with the `cliffhanger` id.
