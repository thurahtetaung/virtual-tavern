# Red Herring

Notes for working on this game. The repo-wide rules are in the root `CLAUDE.md`, including the shared patterns every back-room game follows; this file lists only what this game adds or does differently. Update this file in the same change as any rule, id or asset change.

## Rules and boundaries

- **Red Herring** (`packages/private-games/red-herring`, id `red-herring`) is a picture-card bluffing game for 4 to 10: each round a word-picker plays a card and writes a secret word, everyone but one random Conspirator gets it in a notebook, two laps of cards face up, one talk step and a vote. Like Starlight it has no other name anywhere, back room included: no DB title and no manifest title.
  - **Cards:** the game's own are numbers 1–168 (Classic) and 169–252 (Noir), its art `cards/<nnn>.jpg`; the rest are Reverie's sets, numbered from `OURS_BASE` (1000 + Reverie's number) and drawn from Reverie's storage through `@vt/private-reverie/card`, every card with this game's back. The host picks sets in the shared `ChoicePicker` ("Picture cards": the two decks, then each Reverie set from `cardSetChoices`, Starlight's included); `settings.sets` lists the ids (`base`, `noir`, `ours-<Reverie set id>`; Classic and Noir by default), and `chosenSets` reads a room saved with the old `decks: {base, noir, ours}` switches (`ours` = every Reverie set). Start is refused unless the chosen sets hold a card for every play (`cardsNeeded`); draws stop once the deck is empty, with no reshuffle.
  - **Rules (the owner's rulings, tested in `game.test.ts` and listed in the guide):** once around at 5+ players, twice under 5 or with Twice around; the word-picker's card goes down before the word; the word is free text up to 30 characters; the Conspirator is random among the others (the balanced deal picks among those who have been it least); the word-picker never votes or is accused; votes are secret and revealed together (the open vote in turn is an option); rulebook scoring, "score only a catch" or "just for fun"; ties go to more scoring rounds, then are shared. After the laps comes one talk step (stage `discuss`): the word is shown to everyone, a few lines say what to do (each player says why they played their cards, then the table talks), and only the host starts the vote; the app runs no speaker turns, Done buttons or Act for there, and its one timer is `discussSeconds` (strict: the vote starts). No Discussion skips it. A round saved in the old `explain` stage reads as the talk (`stageOf`), and a saved `talkSeconds` is ignored. The word step's timer is a reminder even in strict mode (nobody's word is written for them). A player who leaves is away: safe moves after `LEFT_BEAT_MS`, never the Conspirator, their word-picker turn skipped (a word-picker leaving before the word drops the round); under 4 present ends the game after the round; the host's `remove` does the same for a dropped player.
  - **`view()`:** your own hand only; the notebook (`{ word }`, `null` for the Conspirator) is the same shape for every role, and its page says the viewer's own role (a Detective's shows the word, the Conspirator's says so, same cover and size); the word reaches the Conspirator and watchers only at the talk step or the reveal; who the Conspirator is reaches nobody before the reveal; secret votes only as "voted"; the deck and the balanced deal's counts never. Tested in `view.test.ts` with a 1,000-game fuzz.
  - **No sound** (the owner's choice): no cues, sound panel or volume settings, and a room saved with `sound`/`hostVolumes` settings ignores them; phones still buzz on their owner's turn (`useTurnBuzz`).
  - **Testing:** `RED_HERRING_TEST_DEAL=<n>` deals in card-number order with the first seat picking first and the Conspirator `n` seats after the word-picker.
  - **Phones:** Table, Me (notebook and hand) and Scores (score list and host tools) on the shared `TabBar`; on your card or play turn the hand is on Table instead. The word form, Start the vote and Next round sit in the fixed `StickyBar` with their refusals. First-time tips are a closed fold on phones and sit at the top of the rail on desktops. With four players the desktop table is one row of seats.

## Assets

**Red Herring** (`node scripts/r2-upload.mjs red-herring <folder>`, dry-run first): the 252 picture cards in `cards/001.jpg`…`cards/252.jpg` (366 × 512; 1–168 the Classic deck, 169–252 the Noir deck), the card back `back.jpg` (shown on every card, our picture cards too, which come from Reverie's storage), the components (`components/lobby-art.jpg`, `notebook-cover.jpg`, `notebook-page.png`, `pencil.png`, the point tokens `vp-3.png`, `vp-4.png`, `vp-5.png` and `vp-back.png`, and the eight board strips `marker-<colour>.jpg`), and a `manifest.json` of the form `{ "back", "sets": { "base": { "name", "from", "to" }, "noir": { … } }, "components": { "lobbyArt", "notebookCover", "notebookPage", "pencil", "vp": { "3", "4", "5", "back" }, "markers": { "blue", "green", "red", "orange", "white", "yellow", "purple", "brown" } }, "guide": { "tagline" }, "credit": "…" or { "title", "paragraphs", "links" } }` (shape in `packages/private-games/red-herring/src/logic/manifest.ts`; `cardSize` and `sameAs` are ignored). Every field is optional: without it, cards are numbered on a plain face, and the components are plain words and emoji. The game has no sound. Red Herring has no title: it keeps its code name everywhere, so set none.
